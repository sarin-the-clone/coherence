
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In a universe on the brink of extinction, the crew of the last starship must find a habitable planet for humanity, battling cosmic forces and their own inner demons to save their species from oblivion.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: The far reaches of space, a diverse array of planets, nebulae, and cosmic ruins, thousands of years after a cataclysmic event nearly wiped out life. Advanced technology, remnants of ancient civilizations, and powerful cosmic forces shape the world.
- Genre blend: Space Opera meets survival drama, with elements of mystery and epic quest
- Tone: Epic, melancholic, and hopeful, with a touch of awe and wonder

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical and introspective, with an emphasis on character emotions and inner turmoil

### Main Throughline
1. [Opening situation: The crew awakens from a centuries-long hibernation, finding their home star dead and their mission suddenly urgent.]
2. [First major complication: A mysterious alien artifact is discovered, offering hope for a habitable planet but also a sinister power that could destroy them all.]
3. [Midpoint reversal: The crew is separated when their ship is attacked by a rogue faction, forcing them to rely on their ingenuity and alliances with other beings to survive.]
4. [Crisis point: The crew must choose between saving humanity and preserving their own lives, as the search for a new home becomes increasingly desperate.]
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state: The crew reunites, finds a planet, and makes a stand against the cosmic forces threatening their survival, forging a new beginning for humanity.]

### Subplots
1. [Subplot A: Captain Amelia grapples with the weight of her responsibility, haunted by past mistakes and fearful of repeating them.]
   - Intersects at: Amelia's choices directly impact the crew's survival and their chances of finding a new home.
2. [Subplot B: Engineer Kai's personal vendetta against the rogue faction drives them to make reckless decisions that risk the crew's safety.]
   - Intersects at: Kai's actions lead to the crew's separation and the loss of valuable resources.
3. [Subplot C: Navigator Aria struggles with feelings of isolation and insignificance, seeking connections with other beings and uncovering secrets about their mission.]
   - Intersects at: Aria's discoveries help the crew navigate dangerous territories and uncover clues about the cataclysmic event that destroyed their home star.
4. [Subplot D: Medical Officer Aiden develops a cure for a mysterious illness afflicting the crew, only to discover it may have deadly consequences for the new planet they find.]
   - Intersects at: Aiden's cure becomes a key bargaining chip in negotiations with other beings, as well as a potential threat to their new home.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The rogue faction, who seek to control the mysterious alien artifact for their own nefarious purposes.
- Secondary obstacles: Cosmic storms, hostile alien creatures, and the ever-present danger of running out of resources.
- Stakes progression: The crew's survival, the fate of humanity, and the balance of power in the universe.
- What's at risk: Personal relationships, individual lives, and the extinction of the human species.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Amelia is burdened by guilt, Kai is driven by anger, Aria is desperate for connection, and Aiden is haunted by the memories of his past failures.
- Core internal conflict: Overcoming personal demons, learning to trust and work together, and finding hope amidst despair.
- Evolution markers: Amelia learns to accept her mistakes and grow as a leader, Kai learns the value of teamwork, Aria finds a sense of purpose, and Aiden faces the consequences of his actions and rededicates himself to saving humanity.
- Ending state: Amelia leads the crew to a new home, Kai finds forgiveness, Aria forges connections with other beings, and Aiden sacrifices himself to ensure the survival of humanity.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: The crew must find a habitable planet, defeat the rogue faction, and establish a new home for humanity.
- Tone of resolution: Bittersweet, as they say goodbye to their old lives and face the unknown.
- Final image/moment: The crew stands on the shores of a new planet, looking up at the stars as they begin to rebuild their lives, carrying with them the memories of their journey and the hope for a brighter future.
