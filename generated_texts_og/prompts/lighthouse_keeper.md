
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In a secluded coastal town, a retired lighthouse keeper stumbles upon a cache of letters in the attic that challenges the accepted truth about his wife's disappearance, leading him on a quest for answers and unearthing long-buried secrets that will shake his world.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: A remote coastal town in the 1960s, where the lighthouse stands as a beacon of solitude amidst the crashing waves and rugged cliffs. The town is steeped in tradition and superstition, with a rich history that hints at a hidden past.
- Genre blend: General Fiction with elements of Mystery and Gothic Romance.
- Tone: Balanced, with a mix of suspense, melancholy, and hope.

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited, following the protagonist's thoughts and emotions.
- Tense: Past tense.
- Voice characteristics: Introspective and descriptive, with a lyrical quality that evokes the haunting beauty of the setting.

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. [Opening situation and inciting incident] As the sun sets on another day, Thomas discovers a mysterious letter hidden in the attic, reigniting memories of his wife's disappearance.
2. [First major complication or discovery] Thomas unearths more letters, revealing a web of lies and deception that questions everything he thought he knew about his wife's fate.
3. [Midpoint reversal or escalation] Thomas discovers a link between his wife's disappearance and a dark secret hidden within the town, forcing him to confront the people he thought he knew and trust.
4. [Crisis point where stakes are highest] Thomas uncovers evidence that puts his own life in danger as he races to expose the truth and clear his name.
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state] Thomas must make a difficult choice between justice and preserving the town's secrets, ultimately deciding to reveal the truth and face the consequences.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. [Subplot A: Thomas's relationship with his daughter] - Intersects at: Thomas reconnects with his estranged daughter, who helps him piece together the puzzle of his wife's disappearance.
2. [Subplot B: The town's superstitions and secrets] - Intersects at: Thomas uncovers the dark history behind the town's legends and learns that the past is not as buried as he thought.
3. [Subplot C: The lighthouse and its connection to the mystery] - Intersects at: Thomas finds clues within the lighthouse that help him understand his wife's involvement in the town's secrets.
4. [Subplot D: Thomas's personal growth and healing] - Intersects at: As Thomas delves deeper into the mystery, he confronts his grief and learns to move forward, ultimately finding a sense of peace.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: A powerful and secretive group within the town that will stop at nothing to protect their hidden past.
- Secondary obstacles: The town's residents, who are unwilling to face the truth and risk exposing their own secrets.
- Stakes progression: As Thomas uncovers more secrets, the stakes grow higher, with his life, his family, and the town's future at risk.
- What's at risk: Thomas's safety, his relationship with his daughter, the town's tradition, and the truth about his wife's disappearance.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Thomas is a reclusive, grief-stricken man, struggling to come to terms with his wife's disappearance and the distance that has grown between him and his daughter.
- Core internal conflict: Thomas must confront his guilt and grief, while also learning to trust others and let go of the past.
- Evolution markers: Thomas reconnects with his daughter, discovers the truth about his wife's disappearance, and learns to forgive those who betrayed him.
- Ending state: Thomas finds peace and closure, while also helping to heal the town and bridge the divide between its past and future.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Thomas must expose the truth about his wife's disappearance and confront the powerful group responsible for her fate.
- Tone of resolution: Bittersweet, with a sense of hope and redemption.
- Final image/moment: Thomas watches as the sun sets over the lighthouse, knowing that he has finally brought justice to his wife's memory and found a way to move forward.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in final 10-15%

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
- **Settings**: Lighthouse, town square, local tavern, coastal cliffs, abandoned house, seaside cave.
- **Scene types**: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, personal growth.
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Crashing waves, fog, lighthouse beacon, loss, grief.
  - Middle third: Whispers, secrets, shadows, trust, redemption.
  - Final third: Hope, light, forgiveness, closure, healing.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.

## Output Rules (Critical for Chunk Stitching)
