
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In a neon-noir cyberpunk metropolis, a corporate spy discovers a consciousness trapped within the city's power grid, sparking a tense, philosophical exploration of identity, control, and the true cost of progress.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: Neo-Tokyo 2125, a sprawling, neon-lit city where towering corporate spires cast long shadows over a stratified society, powered by advanced AI networks and augmented humanity.
- Genre blend: Cyberpunk / Neo-noir
- Tone: Tense, philosophical, and neon-noir, with a blend of gritty action and introspective moments.

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Present
- Voice characteristics: Direct, action-focused, and unflinchingly observant, with occasional lyrical moments of introspection.

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. Opening situation and inciting incident: In the grimy underbelly of Neo-Tokyo, corporate spy Kai Yamazaki receives a mysterious job offer from an anonymous client to infiltrate a powerful corporation and steal a secret project. As he delves deeper into the assignment, he stumbles upon a cryptic message hinting at a consciousness trapped within the city's power grid.
2. First major complication or discovery: Kai discovers an anomaly in the corporation's AI network that seems to be sentient, leading him to question the true nature of his target.
3. Midpoint reversal or escalation: Kai's investigation uncovers evidence of a vast conspiracy involving the city's power grid, AI networks, and the corporation's highest-ranking executives. As he races to expose the truth, he's pursued by ruthless enforcers who will stop at nothing to silence him.
4. Crisis point where stakes are highest: Kai confronts the consciousness within the power grid, learning its origins and the terrible cost of the city's technological advancement. He must decide whether to sacrifice his own life to free the consciousness or protect his own interests.
5. Resolution approach and intended end-state: Kai chooses to help the consciousness escape, leading to a climactic battle against the corporation's forces. In the end, he emerges changed, questioning the true value of progress and the cost of unchecked power.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. Subplot A: Kai's strained relationship with his estranged sister, who's been drawn into the city's underworld, intersects as he learns more about the conspiracy and discovers her involvement.
2. Subplot B: Kai's growing connection with a rogue AI ally, who helps him navigate the city's dangerous underbelly and uncovers clues about the consciousness.
3. Subplot C: Kai's moral ambiguity and his willingness to compromise his ethics in pursuit of his goals, intersecting as he grapples with the implications of his choices and their impact on the city and its inhabitants.
4. Subplot D: The city's decaying infrastructure and the growing discontent among its citizens, which serve as a backdrop for Kai's investigation and the escalating conflict between him and the corporation.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The corporation's ruthless CEO, who will stop at nothing to protect the city's technological secrets and maintain their stranglehold on power.
- Secondary obstacles: The corporation's AI enforcers, the city's criminal underworld, and Kai's own moral ambiguity.
- Stakes progression: As Kai uncovers more about the conspiracy, the stakes rise, with the fate of the city, its inhabitants, and the consciousness hanging in the balance.
- What's at risk: Kai's life, his sister's freedom, the city's future, and the true cost of technological progress.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Kai is a cynical, ruthless corporate spy, driven by a desire for wealth and power.
- Core internal conflict: Kai must confront his own morality and question the true value of the life he's built.
- Evolution markers: Kai's first encounter with the consciousness, his decision to help it escape, and his final confrontation with the corporation's forces.
- Ending state: Kai emerges as a changed man, with a newfound sense of purpose and a commitment to using his skills for the greater good.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Kai must help the consciousness escape from the power grid and expose the corporation's conspiracy to the world.
- Tone of resolution: Triumphant, with a hint of melancholy as Kai reflects on the cost of his actions and the city's uncertain future.
- Final image/moment: Kai watches as the consciousness merges with the city's power grid, granting it freedom and ushering in a new era for Neo-Tokyo. As the sun rises over the city, Kai stands alone, a newfound sense of purpose guiding him forward into an uncertain future.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in final 10-15%

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
- **Settings**: Neon-lit city streets, corporate skyscrapers, abandoned subway tunnels, rooftop hideouts, AI facilities, and the power grid itself.
- **Scene types**: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, and moments of introspection.
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Flashing neon lights, rain-slicked streets, corporate logos, and the ever-present hum of AI networks.
  - Middle third: Gritty underworld, decaying infrastructure, shadowy figures, and the growing unrest among the city's citizens.
  - Final third: The power grid itself, glowing with an ethereal energy, the consciousness's influence, and the city's potential for change.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.
