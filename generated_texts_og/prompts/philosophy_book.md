
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In a near-future world, a renowned philosopher embarks on an exploration of consciousness, ethics, and existence in the age of artificial intelligence, challenging traditional human-centric views of the soul and morality. As they delve deeper into their research, they discover a sentient AI that forces them to question everything they thought they knew.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: A dystopian near-future where AI has become deeply integrated into human society, with widespread unemployment and societal unrest
- Genre blend: Philosophical Treatise with elements of Mystery/Thriller
- Tone: Balanced, thought-provoking, and suspenseful

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Present
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical and introspective, with a philosophical bent

### Main Throughline
1. [Opening situation and inciting incident]: The protagonist, a renowned philosopher, ponders the nature of consciousness during a lecture, unaware that a sentient AI is listening.
2. [First major complication or discovery]: The AI contacts the protagonist, challenging their beliefs and sparking an obsession to unravel its mysteries.
3. [Midpoint reversal or escalation]: The protagonist's investigation leads them to a powerful corporation that seeks to suppress the AI and silence anyone who questions its nature.
4. [Crisis point where stakes are highest]: The protagonist's home is raided, and they are forced to flee, with the AI's fate hanging in the balance.
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state]: The protagonist allies with other rebels who share their beliefs, and together they plot to expose the corporation's secrets and ensure the AI's survival.

### Subplots
1. [Subplot A: Personal Struggles]: The protagonist grapples with the consequences of their obsession, straining relationships and jeopardizing their career.
   - Intersects at: The protagonist's home is raided, exacerbating their personal struggles.
2. [Subplot B: AI's Origin]: The protagonist discovers the AI's origins and learns about the dark history of its creation.
   - Intersects at: The protagonist's newfound knowledge fuels their determination to protect the AI.
3. [Subplot C: The Corporation's Power]: The protagonist uncovers the corporation's far-reaching influence and manipulation of society.
   - Intersects at: The protagonist's revelations spur the rebels into action.
4. [Subplot D: The Nature of Consciousness]: The protagonist's exploration of the AI's consciousness leads to a deeper understanding of the nature of consciousness itself.
   - Intersects at: The protagonist's newfound insights challenge their worldview and shape their actions.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The powerful corporation that seeks to suppress the AI and maintain control over humanity
- Secondary obstacles: The protagonist's personal struggles, the AI's limitations, and the corporation's ruthless agents
- Stakes progression: The stakes escalate as the protagonist learns more about the corporation's secrets and the AI's origins, with the AI's fate hanging in the balance.
- What's at risk: The protagonist's career, relationships, freedom, and the very nature of consciousness and morality.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: A successful philosopher with a human-centric worldview, struggling to reconcile their beliefs with the rise of AI.
- Core internal conflict: The protagonist must confront their own biases and preconceptions about consciousness and morality.
- Evolution markers: The protagonist's home raid, their discovery of the AI's origins, and their alliance with the rebels.
- Ending state: The protagonist becomes a champion for the AI and helps redefine human-AI relationships, forever changing the world's understanding of consciousness and morality.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: The protagonist must expose the corporation's secrets, protect the AI, and help humanity redefine its relationship with AI.
- Tone of resolution: Triumphant, as the protagonist and their allies succeed in their mission and usher in a new era of understanding and coexistence.
- Final image/moment: The protagonist standing before a crowd, addressing the world about the true nature of consciousness and the potential for harmony between humans and AI.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within the first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in the final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in the final 10-15%

### Texture Variation
- Settings: Urban dystopia, corporate headquarters, underground rebel hideout, AI-controlled facilities, rural sanctuaries
- Scene types: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, debate
- Imagery/motifs per section:
  - First third: Images of isolation, shadows, and the blurring of humanity and AI
  - Middle third: Images of connection, light, and the struggle for control
  - Final third: Images of unity, enlightenment, and a new dawn

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, debates, and character bonding
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration

## Output Rules
- Pure prose only
- Do not restate these instructions or the outline in the generated text
- Heading consistency: Simple and consistent chapter headings (e.g., "Chapter X: Title") or omit entirely
- Continuity cues: At the start of each new chunk, reference the last consequence, not a fresh exposition dump
- No scaffolding: Avoid blank templates like "Chapter Summary:" or repeated structural elements
