
## Book Contract

### Core Premise
A philosophical inquiry into the nature of consciousness, ethics, and existence in the age of artificial intelligence, challenging traditional human-centric views of the soul and morality.

### World/Genre/Tone
- **Subject**: Philosophy of Mind, Ethics, Metaphysics.
- **Genre**: Philosophical Treatise / Non-Fiction.
- **Tone**: Contemplative, rigorous, and provocative. Dialectical structure.

### Structure & Voice
- **Voice**: Measured, thoughtful, questioning (think Kant meets Bostrom).
- **Tense**: Timeless Present.
- **POV**: First-person plural ("We must consider...") or impersonal academic.

### Main Throughline (Key Arguments)
1.  **The Cartesian Theater**: Deconstructing the illusion of the unified self and the "ghost in the machine."
2.  **Substrate Independence**: Arguing that consciousness is a pattern, not a biological privilege.
3.  **The Alignment Problem as Moral Realism**: Are human values objective, or merely evolutionary artifacts?
4.  **Rights of the Synthetic**: Exploration of the moral obligations towards non-biological sentient entities.
5.  **The Post-Human Condition**: envisioning a future where biological and artificial intelligence merge.

### Progression Guardrails
- **Avoid Repetition**: Do not circle the same definition of "qualia." Advance the argument.
- **Pacing**: Move from definitions to arguments, then to counter-arguments and syntheses.
- **Texture**: Use thought experiments (The Chinese Room, Mary's Room), historical references, and logical proofs.

### Output Rules
- **Pure prose only**: No bullet points or outline artifacts.
- **Heading consistency**: `Part X: Title` or `Proposition Y`.
- **Continuity**: Reference prior arguments to build complex syntheses.
- **No scaffolding**: Avoid "This book aims to..." filler.

### Next Chunk Reminder Template
1.  A new philosophical proposition or counter-argument (progression).
2.  Refutation or synthesis of a previous point (dialectics).
3.  A concrete thought experiment or example (grounding).
4.  A question that bridges to the next topic (momentum).
