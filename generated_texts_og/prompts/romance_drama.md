## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In a bustling modern city, a dedicated architect and a free-spirited musician clash over the renovation of a historic theater, only to find their opposing worldviews might be the key to both saving the building and healing their own past wounds.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: A vibrant metropolis with a mix of old-world charm and modern glass skyscrapers. The focus is on the "The Golden Era" theater, a crumbling but majestic art deco landmark.
- Genre blend: Contemporary Romance meets Workplace Drama
- Tone: Warm, witty, and emotional, with moments of deep vulnerability.

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited (alternating chapters between the two leads)
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Introspective and grounded for the architect; lyrical and sensory-focused for the musician.

### Main Throughline
1. [Opening situation: Elias (Architect) is hired to modernize the theater, while Clara (Musician/Activist) leads a protest to preserve its original soul.]
2. [First major complication: They are forced to co-chair the community committee, leading to heated arguments but also grudging respect.]
3. [Midpoint reversal: A structural failure threatens to condemn the building entirely. They must combine Elias's engineering and Clara's fundraising concerts to save it.]
4. [Crisis point: A secret from Elias's firm reveals they plan to demolish it anyway. Elias must choose between his career and the theater (and Clara).]
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state: They expose the firm, rally the city, and renovate the theater as a fusion of old and new. They admit their feelings.]

### Subplots
1. [Subplot A: Clara's estranged father was the theater's original conductor.]
2. [Subplot B: Elias's rivalry with a younger, cutthroat architect.]
3. [Subplot C: The ghost of the theater (metaphorical or ambiguous) guiding them.]

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist: The greedy development firm and time/decay.
- Stakes: The loss of cultural heritage, career ruin, and the chance at true love.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Saving the theater and the union of the couple.
- Tone of resolution: Joyful and celebratory.

## Progression Guardrails
- Ensure emotional beats land before plot twists.
- Keep the renovation progress visible.
