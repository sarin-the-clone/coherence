
## Story Contract

### Core Premise

A captivating odyssey through the evolution of the universe, delving into the origins of matter, tracing the Big Bang's aftermath, and exploring the formation of complex life as we know it, all presented in a balanced tone that invites both curiosity and contemplation.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: Timeless, traversing the cosmos, spanning across celestial bodies and eras; the technology level is that of scientific observation and theoretical models
- Genre blend: Popular Science / Cosmic Odyssey
- Tone: Balanced

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Present
- Voice characteristics: Inquisitive, thoughtful, and accessible

### Main Throughline
1. The narrative begins with an immersive exploration of the Big Bang's aftermath, as seen through the eyes of a hypothetical observer.
2. The first major complication arises when our observer encounters the formation of the first stars, unraveling the mysteries of their birth and evolution.
3. The midpoint reversal occurs as the observer witnesses the birth of galaxies and the expansion of the universe, questioning the origins of the cosmic structure.
4. The crisis point emerges as the observer delves into the origins of Earth and the formation of life, grappling with the complexity and interconnectedness of the natural world.
5. The resolution approach centers around the observer's exploration of the emergence of complex life on Earth, seeking answers to the enigma of consciousness and the nature of existence.

### Subplots
1. Subplot A: The Evolution of Matter - Intersects at: The observer's detailed exploration of the various stages of matter's evolution, from subatomic particles to complex organisms.
2. Subplot B: The Observer's Personal Journey - Intersects at: The observer's own self-discovery as they grapple with the vastness and complexity of the universe, questioning their place within it.
3. Subplot C: The Scientific Community's Role - Intersects at: The observer's examination of the scientific community's role in understanding the universe, revealing both the triumphs and limitations of human knowledge.
4. Subplot D: The Philosophical Implications - Intersects at: The observer's contemplation of the philosophical and metaphysical implications of the origins of the universe, delving into questions of purpose and meaning.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The unknown - the vastness, complexity, and incomprehensibility of the universe, which constantly challenges the observer's understanding and forces them to adapt and grow.
- Secondary obstacles: The limitations of human knowledge, the observer's personal biases and preconceptions, and the challenges posed by scientific theories that contradict one another.
- Stakes progression: The stakes escalate as the observer delves deeper into the mysteries of the universe, facing greater challenges to their understanding and confronting the enormity of the questions they seek to answer.
- What's at risk: The observer's sanity, their sense of self, and the very foundations of human knowledge.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: The observer is an expert in the field of cosmology, driven by an insatiable curiosity and a desire to understand the universe's mysteries. They are initially confident in their understanding but are soon confronted with the enormity of the questions they must answer.
- Core internal conflict: The observer must confront their own limitations, biases, and assumptions as they delve deeper into the mysteries of the universe.
- Evolution markers: The observer experiences moments of revelation and self-discovery, adjusting their perspective and understanding of the universe.
- Ending state: The observer emerges with a newfound appreciation for the complexity and interconnectedness of the universe, having gained a deeper understanding of the origins of matter and the emergence of life.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: The observer must unravel the enigma of the universe's origins, delving into the formation of complex life and seeking answers to the questions of consciousness and purpose.
- Tone of resolution: Triumphant, with a sense of awe and wonder at the beauty and complexity of the universe.
- Final image/moment: The observer gazes out at the cosmos, having gained a deeper understanding of its mysteries, and is filled with a sense of awe and humility at the enormity of the universe and the intricate tapestry of life that has emerged within it.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within the first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in the final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in the final 10-15%

### Texture Variation
- **Settings**: Celestial bodies (stars, galaxies, planets), distant nebulae, laboratory settings, deserts, forests, oceans
- **Scene types**: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, dialogue, monologue
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Cosmic imagery (stars, galaxies, nebulae), scientific terminology, questions and exploration
  - Middle third: Personal growth, introspection, self-discovery, philosophical contemplation, cosmological connections
  - Final third: Cosmic awareness, awe, humility, connection with life, questions of purpose and meaning

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration

## Output Rules
- **Pure prose only** - no bullet lists, JSON, or meta-notes in the actual story
- **Do not restate** these instructions or the outline in the generated text
- **Heading consistency**: If using chapter headings, keep format simple and consistent (e.g., `Chapter X: Title`) or omit entirely; never change heading style mid-book
- **Continuity cues**: At the start of each new chunk, reference the last consequence, not a fresh exposition dump
- **No scaffolding**: Avoid blank templates like "Chapter Summary:" or repeated structural elements

1. A new event (not a rehash)
2. A consequence of any earlier event (callback/continuity)
