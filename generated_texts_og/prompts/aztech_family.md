
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In the Aztec Empire, the rise and fall of a powerful dynasty unfolds through the eyes of the Tlaloc family, as they navigate political intrigue, religious strife, and the ever-shifting tides of destiny, ultimately shaping the fate of their civilization.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: Thirteenth-century Tenochtitlan, a thriving metropolis built on a series of islands in Lake Texcoco, filled with grand temples, marketplaces, and palaces. The world is a blend of historical accuracy, rich mythology, and epic adventure.
- Genre blend: Historical Drama with elements of Mystery and Romance.
- Tone: Adventurous, Epic, and Sweeping, with moments of melancholy and profound introspection.

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited, following the perspective of the Tlaloc family's heir, Xochitl.
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical, introspective, and grounded, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human heart.

### Main Throughline
1. Xochitl, a humble scribe's daughter, is thrust into the political limelight when her father uncovers a long-lost prophecy that foretells the rise of a new dynasty.
2. Xochitl discovers her own connection to the prophecy and begins to learn the true extent of her family's influence and power.
3. Midpoint reversal: Xochitl's family is targeted by a rival dynasty, forcing her to make a dangerous alliance with an enigmatic priestess and a disgraced warrior.
4. Crisis point: Xochitl is betrayed, and her family's power is on the brink of collapse. She must navigate a series of trials to reclaim her birthright and save her people from certain doom.
5. Resolution approach: Xochitl leads a rebellion against the ruling class, seeking to restore balance and unity to the Aztec Empire.

### Subplots
1. Subplot A: Xochitl's forbidden romance with a nobleman, who helps her navigate the treacherous political landscape. Intersects at: Xochitl's growing feelings for him create tension and conflict within the Tlaloc family and the nobility.
2. Subplot B: The mysterious origins of the prophecy, and the role it plays in the rise and fall of empires. Intersects at: Xochitl's exploration of her family's past reveals a darker history than she could have imagined.
3. Subplot C: The political machinations of the rival dynasty, as they seek to maintain their grip on power. Intersects at: Xochitl uncovers their true intentions and must outmaneuver them at every turn.
4. Subplot D: The religious tensions within the Aztec Empire, as different factions vie for control of the people's hearts and minds. Intersects at: Xochitl must navigate the complex web of religious politics to secure the support she needs for her rebellion.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The ruling class, who fear the rise of the Tlaloc dynasty and will stop at nothing to maintain their power.
- Secondary obstacles: The enigmatic priestess, who has her own agenda and may not be a true ally; the disgraced warrior, whose own past sins threaten to undermine their alliance; and the rival dynasty, who seek to destroy the Tlaloc family and seize power for themselves.
- Stakes progression: The stakes escalate as Xochitl's family's power grows, drawing the ire of the ruling class and the rival dynasty. The consequences of failure are dire, as the Aztec Empire teeters on the brink of chaos and destruction.
- What's at risk: Xochitl's family's legacy, the fate of the Aztec Empire, and the lives of countless innocents.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Xochitl is a sheltered, intelligent young woman with a deep love for her family and a keen sense of justice. She is unsure of her place in the world, but determined to make a difference.
- Core internal conflict: Xochitl must learn to trust herself and her own instincts, even when those around her doubt her.
- Evolution markers: Xochitl's first major act of defiance, her betrayal, and her ultimate triumph over the ruling class.
- Ending state: Xochitl has become a powerful leader, respected by her people and feared by her enemies. She has learned the true cost of power and the importance of compassion and empathy.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Xochitl must reclaim her birthright, overthrow the ruling class, and restore balance to the Aztec Empire.
- Tone of resolution: Triumphant, with a touch of melancholy as Xochitl reflects on the sacrifices she's made and the loved ones she's lost along the way.
- Final image/moment: Xochitl stands on the steps of the Great Temple, overlooking the city she has saved, surrounded by her family and allies. The sun sets, casting long shadows over the landscape, as the people of the Aztec Empire look to their future with hope.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter must introduce a new obstacle or consequence, never looping back to a previous state.
- Reuse prior events as consequences, not reintroduced as new discoveries.
- Shift settings and scene types periodically, avoiding identical openings and repetitive scene structures.
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed, prioritizing forward momentum and decisions.
- Keep character goals evolving, replacing one goal with a sharper one when the previous one is achieved or failed.

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within the first 5% of text.
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%.
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%.
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in the final 25%.
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in the final 10-15%.

### Texture Variation
- Settings: Marketplaces, temples, palaces, forests, battlefields, and the homes of various characters.
- Scene types: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, and quiet moments of introspection.
- Imagery/motifs per section:
  - First third: Imagery of water, flowers, and birds, symbolizing growth and renewal.
  - Middle third: Imagery of fire, smoke, and shadows, symbolizing danger and transformation.
  - Final third: Imagery of the sun, the moon, and stars, symbolizing hope and the passage of time.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, and character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, and world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.

## Output Rules

- Pure prose only - no bullet lists, JSON, or meta-notes in the actual story.
- Do not restate these instructions or the outline in the generated text.
- Headings consistency: If using chapter headings, keep format simple and consistent (e.g., "Chapter X: Title") or omit entirely; never change heading style mid-book.
- Continuity cues: At the start of each new chunk, reference the last consequence, not a fresh exposition dump.
- No scaffolding: Avoid blank templates like "Chapter Summary:" or repeated structural elements.
