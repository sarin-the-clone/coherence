
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In the twilight of an ancient empire, a family of scholars embarks on a centuries-long quest to safeguard a lost library, facing myriad challenges as they navigate the shifting sands of power, war, and discovery.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: The crumbling Silk Road empire of Zephyr, encompassing vast deserts, bustling city-states, and mysterious mountain fortresses, during the tumultuous transition from ancient wisdom to medieval superstition.
- Genre blend: Historical fiction with elements of adventure, mystery, and epic historical drama.
- Tone: Epic and scholarly, with a rich, immersive sense of time and place, interwoven with intellectual intrigue and moments of heart-stopping action.

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited, following the perspective of the family's matriarch, scholar-priestess Aria.
- Tense: Past.
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical and introspective, with a keen eye for detail and a subtle, evocative prose style.

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. [Opening situation and inciting incident]: Aria, a gifted scribe and seeker of knowledge, uncovers the existence of an ancient library hidden deep within the sands of Zephyr. The library contains the collective wisdom of the empire's founders, lost for millennia.
2. [First major complication or discovery]: Aria learns that the library is not only hidden but protected by powerful enchantments, meant to preserve its secrets and keep them out of the hands of those who would misuse the knowledge it contains.
3. [Midpoint reversal or escalation]: Aria and her family are forced to flee their home as the empire crumbles around them, pursued by ruthless warlords who seek the library's power for their own gain.
4. [Crisis point where stakes are highest]: The family arrives at the library's location, only to find it under attack by a fanatical cult intent on destroying the ancient texts. Aria must rally her family and confront the cult's leader in a battle to save the library and its secrets.
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state]: With the library secured, Aria and her family face the challenge of restoring and preserving its knowledge for future generations, as they navigate the treacherous waters of a fractured empire.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. [Subplot A: Family dynamics]: Aria's children, each with unique talents and personalities, must learn to work together and overcome their internal conflicts as they face the challenges of their quest. This subplot intersects at key moments of crisis, forcing the family to confront their weaknesses and grow stronger together.
2. [Subplot B: The changing world]: As the empire collapses and new powers rise, Aria and her family grapple with the shifting sands of politics, religion, and culture. This subplot intersects as the family encounters various factions vying for control, and must navigate the complex web of alliances and enmities that define their world.
3. [Subplot C: The search for allies]: Aria and her family seek out other scholars and seekers of knowledge who might aid them in their quest to preserve the library. This subplot intersects as the family forms temporary alliances and uneasy friendships, each with their own motivations and secrets.
4. [Subplot D: The hidden knowledge]: As the family delves deeper into the library's secrets, they uncover long-lost truths about the empire's history, the origins of the enchantments protecting the library, and the true nature of the knowledge it contains. This subplot intersects as the family's discoveries impact their decisions and the course of their journey.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The fanatical cult seeking to destroy the library and its secrets, driven by a twisted interpretation of the ancient wisdom it contains.
- Secondary obstacles: Warlords and factions who seek the library's power for their own ends, as well as the harsh desert environment and the ever-present threat of betrayal.
- Stakes progression: The stakes escalate as the family faces increasingly dangerous foes, and the library's secrets become more valuable and more vulnerable.
- What's at risk: The survival of the family, the preservation of the library's knowledge, and the future of Zephyr itself.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Aria is a dedicated scholar and mother, driven by a deep love of knowledge and a desire to protect her family. She is skilled in both the academic and the practical, a master of the written word and a capable fighter.
- Core internal conflict: Aria must reconcile her duty to preserve the library's knowledge with her desire to protect her family, as the two often come into conflict.
- Evolution markers: Aria's growth is marked by her increasing willingness to take risks and make difficult decisions, as well as her growing understanding of the true nature of the library's secrets.
- Ending state: Aria emerges as a wise and resilient leader, having weathered countless storms and faced insurmountable odds to protect the library and her family.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: The library must be restored, its secrets protected, and its knowledge preserved for future generations.
- Tone of resolution: Bittersweet, as Aria and her family face the reality of a changed world and the knowledge that their journey is not yet over.
- Final image/moment: Aria, surrounded by her family and the ancient tomes of the library, gazes out over the sands of Zephyr as the sun sets, a testament to the enduring power of knowledge and the indomitable spirit of those who seek it.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in final 10-15%

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
- **Settings**: Desert oases, bustling city-states, mountain fortresses, ruined temples, hidden libraries, war-torn battlefields, treacherous mountain passes.
- **Scene types**: Confrontations, discoveries, travel, introspection, action, negotiation, revelation, reflection, escape, alliance-building.
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Sands, stars, fire, ancient ruins, secrets, family bonds.
  - Middle third: Cities, politics, religion, culture, alliances, betrayal, knowledge.
  - Final third: Libraries, enchantments, power, the changing world, the future.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.
