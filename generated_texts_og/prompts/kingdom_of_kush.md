
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In the Kingdom of Kush (Nubia), a blacksmith named Amani forges alliances to outshine declining Egypt, uncovering secrets that could tip the balance of power in the ancient world.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: East Africa, 650 BC, bustling iron-working city-states along the Nile, rich in culture and trade
- Genre blend: Historical Fiction with elements of Adventure
- Tone: Epic, Detailed, Atmospheric

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Direct, observant, and poetic, with a touch of melancholy

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. Amani, a skilled blacksmith, discovers an ancient artifact in the Nubian desert, igniting a rivalry with Egyptian pharaohs.
2. Amani learns of a secret iron-working technique from an enigmatic traveler, enhancing his reputation and attracting unwanted attention.
3. Amani's city is ambushed by Egyptians, forcing him to seek help from rival Nubian city-states and uncover hidden alliances.
4. Amani unearths a lost Nubian city, where he discovers the true origin of the artifact and a powerful weapon that could change the balance of power.
5. Amani must navigate treacherous political waters to wield the weapon against the Egyptians, risking everything for the future of his people.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. Subplot A: Amani's love for a captured Egyptian priestess complicates his loyalties and exposes him to new perspectives. - Intersects at: Amani's growing doubts about the righteousness of his quest.
2. Subplot B: A mysterious sorceress helps Amani on his journey, offering guidance and protection in exchange for his allegiance. - Intersects at: Amani's decision to use the weapon, with the sorceress urging caution.
3. Subplot C: Amani's younger sister, Nefret, follows him on his travels, seeking her own adventure and challenging Amani's leadership. - Intersects at: Nefret's capture by the Egyptians, forcing Amani to choose between his quest and rescuing his sister.
4. Subplot D: A rebellious young pharaoh seeks to usurp the throne and align with Nubia, creating an unexpected ally for Amani. - Intersects at: Amani's alliance with the young pharaoh, turning the tide against the ruling Egyptian council.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: Egyptian Pharaohs, who seek to maintain their dominance and suppress the rising power of Nubia
- Secondary obstacles:
  - Nubian city-states that distrust Amani and his powerful weapon
  - Political intrigue within the Egyptian court, as factions vie for power
  - Amani's inner turmoil and doubts about the righteousness of his quest

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: A skilled blacksmith with a strong sense of duty to his people, but limited political acumen and worldly experience
- Core internal conflict: Amani must reconcile his desire for personal growth and self-preservation with the needs of his people and the demands of his quest
- Evolution markers:
  - Amani's realization of the true power and potential of the weapon
  - Amani's growth as a leader, learning to balance the needs of his people with his personal desires
  - Amani's acceptance of his love for the Egyptian priestess and the complexities of the world beyond his city

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Amani must use the weapon to defeat the Egyptians, securing a new era of prosperity for the Kingdom of Kush
- Tone of resolution: Triumphant, with a sense of bittersweet reflection on the sacrifices made along the way
- Final image/moment: Amani, standing atop a Nubian pyramid, gazing out over a united and thriving Africa, surrounded by his allies and loved ones

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter must introduce a new obstacle or consequence, without looping back to the status quo
- Reuse prior events as consequences, but avoid reintroducing the same discovery or conflict
- Shift settings and scene types periodically, avoiding repetitive openings and structures
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving, replacing them with sharper objectives when one is achieved or failed

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within the first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in the final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in the final 10-15%

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
- Settings:
  - The bustling iron-working city of Kerma
  - The rugged Nubian desert
  - The opulent Egyptian court
  - The hidden, overgrown Nubian city
  - The battlefield between the two great powers
- Scene types:
  - Action scenes, as Amani and his allies fight against the Egyptians
  - Discovery scenes, where Amani uncovers new information or artifacts
  - Reflection scenes, where Amani contemplates his choices and doubts
  - Negotiation scenes, as Amani seeks alliances and makes deals
  - Travel scenes, as Amani and his companions journey between cities
- Imagery/motifs per section:
  - First third: The heat of the desert, the strength of iron, the beauty of the Nile
  - Middle third: The shadows of the Egyptian court, the allure of power, the complexity of love
  - Final third: The rising sun over a united Africa, the weight of responsibility, the power of unity

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, and character bonding moments
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel scenes, introspection, and world-building
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration
