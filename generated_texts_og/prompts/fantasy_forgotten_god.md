
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In the mystical realm of Eldoria, a forgotten god awakens in a mortal body, struggling to remember their divine purpose while battling the encroaching darkness that threatens to consume the world.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: A richly-detailed world where ancient magic, mythical creatures, and mysterious relics permeate the land; time period is a golden age of peace, but darkness looms on the horizon.
- Genre blend: High fantasy with mythical undertones
- Tone: Mythic, lyrical, and tragic

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical and introspective, evoking the divine within a mortal vessel

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. [Opening situation and inciting incident]: Our protagonist, Lyrion, finds himself in a forgotten temple, unable to remember who he is or how he arrived. He discovers a prophecy that speaks of a forgotten god destined to save the world from an impending darkness.
2. [First major complication or discovery]: Lyrion encounters a group of villagers who share the prophecy and believe he is the god they've been waiting for. However, he is also pursued by an ancient order dedicated to keeping the forgotten gods locked away, fearing their power.
3. [Midpoint reversal or escalation]: Lyrion learns that the darkness he must defeat is his own people, the Eldorians, who have been driven mad by a curse and now seek to destroy the world.
4. [Crisis point where stakes are highest]: Lyrion discovers the means to break the curse, but it requires him to sacrifice his newfound mortal life.
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state]: Lyrion makes the difficult choice, embracing his divine power to save the world while accepting his mortal fate.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. [Subplot A: A mortal companion]: Lyrion meets a mortal woman named Elysia, who joins him on his journey to uncover his past and save the world. She provides a relatable perspective and challenges Lyrion's divine detachment. Intersects at: Elysia helps Lyrion remember his past and confronts the darkness within him.
2. [Subplot B: A forbidden power]: Lyrion discovers an ancient artifact that holds the power to break the curse but risks unleashing an even greater evil. Intersects at: Lyrion must choose between his own survival and the fate of the world.
3. [Subplot C: A hidden enemy]: A member of the ancient order poses as a loyal ally, manipulating Lyrion and sabotaging his efforts to save the world. Intersects at: Lyrion uncovers the traitor's deception, leading to a confrontation that tests his resolve.
4. [Subplot D: A fractured world]: As Lyrion travels the land, he encounters various Eldorian factions, each with their own interpretations of the prophecy and motivations for supporting or opposing him. Intersects at: Lyrion must navigate the political landscape and make allies to achieve his goal.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: The mad Eldorians, driven by a curse that consumes their souls, seek to bring about the end of the world.
- Secondary obstacles: The ancient order dedicated to keeping forgotten gods locked away, the forbidden power contained within the artifact, and Lyrion's own struggle to accept his divine destiny.
- Stakes progression: As Lyrion delves deeper into his past, the darkness within him grows stronger, putting both his own life and the fate of the world at risk.
- What's at risk: Lyrion's mortal life, the survival of the world, and the balance between the divine and mortal realms.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: Lyrion is a god without memories, struggling to reconcile his divine nature with his mortal existence.
- Core internal conflict: Lyrion must learn to accept his destiny as a god, while also finding meaning in his mortal life.
- Evolution markers: Lyrion's interactions with Elysia, his encounters with the various Eldorian factions, and his confrontation with the traitor within the ancient order.
- Ending state: Lyrion accepts his divine destiny, sacrificing his mortal life to save the world and finding peace in the knowledge that his actions will be remembered by those he saves.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Lyrion must break the curse that has driven the Eldorians mad and restore balance to the world.
- Tone of resolution: Bittersweet, as Lyrion accepts his mortal fate while achieving his divine purpose.
- Final image/moment: Lyrion, surrounded by the people he has saved, ascends to the heavens, leaving behind a world forever changed by his actions.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter/segment must change the situation: new obstacle or consequence; no loops or "back to normal"
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...")
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within first 5% of text
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in final 25%
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in final 10-15%

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
Rotate these elements to keep scenes feeling fresh:
- **Settings**: Forests, mountains, cities, ruins, underwater realms, and ancient temples.
- **Scene types**: Confrontation, discovery, travel, reflection, action, negotiation, and introspection.
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Gods, stars, light, and the color gold.
  - Middle third: Shadows, darkness, fear, and the color red.
  - Final third: Hope, rebirth, transformation, and the color silver.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.
