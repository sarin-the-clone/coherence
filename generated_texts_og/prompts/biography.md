
## Story Contract

### Core Premise
In the late 19th century, the brilliant but reclusive inventor, Dr. Ada Lovelace, revolutionizes energy transmission, only to disappear mysteriously, leaving behind a world of wonder and a trail of unanswered questions.

### World/Genre/Tone
- Setting: Victorian England, with its intricate social hierarchy and industrial advancements, juxtaposed against Dr. Lovelace's secret laboratory hidden within the heart of the city.
- Genre blend: Historical biography with elements of mystery and science fiction
- Tone: Balanced, combining the elegance of the era with the thrill of discovery and the enigma of the unknown

### POV and Tense
- Point of view: Third-person limited
- Tense: Past
- Voice characteristics: Lyrical and introspective, capturing the essence of Dr. Lovelace's genius and solitude

### Main Throughline (4-5 concrete beats)
1. [Opening situation and inciting incident]: As the sun rises over foggy London, Dr. Lovelace unveils her groundbreaking energy transmission device, capturing the attention of the scientific community and the city's elite.
2. [First major complication or discovery]: When Dr. Lovelace's invention attracts unwanted attention from those seeking to exploit her technology, she retreats into her secret laboratory, her isolation deepening.
3. [Midpoint reversal or escalation]: In the midst of her research, Dr. Lovelace stumbles upon a mysterious artifact that hints at a hidden world of energy and technology beyond her wildest dreams.
4. [Crisis point where stakes are highest]: As her work progresses, Dr. Lovelace becomes increasingly obsessed with the artifact, jeopardizing her relationships and her safety as she uncovers a conspiracy that threatens the entire city.
5. [Resolution approach and intended end-state]: In a climactic confrontation, Dr. Lovelace must decide whether to reveal the truth about her discoveries and the conspiracy, or to continue her work in secret, potentially risking her own life.

### Subplots (3-4 intersecting arcs)
1. [Subplot A: The Race for Energy Dominance]: As Dr. Lovelace works on her invention, rival inventors and industrialists seek to claim her work for themselves, setting the stage for a high-stakes competition that threatens to consume the city.
2. [Subplot B: Dr. Lovelace's Struggle with Solitude]: As her reclusiveness grows, Dr. Lovelace finds herself increasingly isolated from the outside world, grappling with the consequences of her choices and the loneliness they bring.
3. [Subplot C: The Mysterious Artifact]: The artifact Dr. Lovelace discovers in her laboratory holds secrets that could change the course of history, but its origins remain shrouded in mystery, driving Dr. Lovelace to delve deeper into the hidden world of energy and technology.
4. [Subplot D: The Conspiracy]: As Dr. Lovelace uncovers more about the conspiracy surrounding her work, she must navigate a web of deception and betrayal, ultimately forcing her to confront the true nature of power and ambition.

### Antagonistic Forces and Stakes
- Primary antagonist/force: A powerful industrialist who will stop at nothing to claim Dr. Lovelace's work for himself, threatening to exploit her invention for personal gain.
- Secondary obstacles: Dr. Lovelace's own isolation, the societal expectations placed upon her as a woman in science, and the complex web of alliances and rivalries within the city's scientific community.
- Stakes progression: The stakes escalate as Dr. Lovelace's work becomes increasingly influential, drawing the attention of those who seek to control it.
- What's at risk: Dr. Lovelace's life, her reputation, and the future of her groundbreaking technology.

### Character Arc Anchors
- Protagonist's starting state: A brilliant but reclusive inventor, driven by her passion for discovery and her desire to leave a lasting impact on the world.
- Core internal conflict: Dr. Lovelace must confront her isolation and the consequences it has on her relationships and her work, ultimately deciding whether to embrace her solitude or seek connection with others.
- Evolution markers: Dr. Lovelace's relationships with her mentor, her rival, and a mysterious stranger who enters her life, each challenging her perceptions of herself and the world around her.
- Ending state: A changed woman, Dr. Lovelace emerges from her isolation with a newfound understanding of the power of connection and the importance of standing up for what she believes in.

### Ending Goal
- What must be resolved: Dr. Lovelace must decide whether to reveal the truth about her discoveries, the conspiracy, and her mysterious artifact, or to continue her work in secret.
- Tone of resolution: Bittersweet, as Dr. Lovelace grapples with the consequences of her choices and the sacrifices she has made.
- Final image/moment: Dr. Lovelace, standing at the edge of her laboratory, gazing out at the city she has both shaped and been shaped by, her future uncertain but filled with hope and promise.

## Progression Guardrails

### Avoid Repetition
- Each chapter must introduce a new obstacle or consequence, without reintroducing the same discovery or conflict.
- Reuse prior events as consequences; do not reintroduce the same discovery/conflict twice.
- Shift settings and scene types periodically; avoid reusing identical openings (e.g., "As the sun rose...").
- Limit recap to one brief line if needed; prioritize forward motion and decisions.
- Keep character goals evolving; when one goal is achieved/failed, replace it with a sharper one.

### Pacing Targets
- Early intrigue: Hook within the first 5% of text.
- First act turn: Major shift by 25%.
- Midpoint reversal: Significant change in direction or stakes at 50%.
- Accelerating decisions: Faster pacing in the final 25%.
- Climax positioning: Peak tension in the final 10-15%.

### Texture Variation (to maintain distinctness)
- **Settings**: Victorian London, Dr. Lovelace's secret laboratory, industrial factories, high society gatherings, mysterious underground locations.
- **Scene types**: Confrontations, discoveries, travel, introspection, action, negotiation.
- **Imagery/motifs per section**:
  - First third: Steam, fog, intricate machinery, shadows, isolation.
  - Middle third: Light, fire, secrets, alliances, deception, power.
  - Final third: Hope, connection, transformation, uncertainty, promise.

### Dialogue/Narration Balance
- Dialogue-heavy scenes: Confrontations, revelations, character bonding.
- Narration-heavy scenes: Travel, introspection, world-building.
- Target ratio: 40% dialogue / 60% narration.

## Output Rules (Critical for Chunk Stitching)

- **Pure prose only** - no bullet lists, JSON, or meta-notes in the actual story.
- **Do not restate** these instructions or the outline in the generated text.
- **Heading consistency**: If using chapter headings, keep format simple and consistent (e.g., "Chapter X: Title") or omit entirely; never change heading style mid-book.
- **Continuity cues**: At the start of each new chunk, reference the last consequence, not a fresh exposition dump.
- **No scaffolding**: Avoid blank templates like "Chapter Summary:" or repeated structural elements.
